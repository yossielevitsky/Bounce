About Bounce

Bounce is a program supporting children and young adults with additional needs within the Antwerp Jewish community.

Our programs provide children with a safe, nurturing and enjoyable environment where they can build confidence, develop new skills, create friendships and simply enjoy being themselves. Every activity is thoughtfully adapted to meet each child's individual needs, strengths and interests, allowing every participant to experience success in their own way.

At Bounce, we recognise that when we support a child, we are also supporting their family. We understand the unique journey that many families experience and strive to create a welcoming community where parents, siblings and families feel understood, connected and supported.

Our team and dedicated volunteers are committed to providing meaningful experiences, genuine care and lasting memories for every child who joins the Bounce family.

Bounce is a volunteer organisation for children with special needs, supported by Tikvatenoe.
