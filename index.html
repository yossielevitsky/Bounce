Welcome to Bounce

Bounce is a voluntary organisation supporting children and young people with mental and physical disabilities, as well as their families.

We provide a safe and inclusive environment where children and young adults can have fun, build friendships, develop confidence, discover new things and create lasting memories together.

Bounce is also here to support parents, siblings and families by providing opportunities to connect with others, spend quality time together and enjoy the respite and support they need.
